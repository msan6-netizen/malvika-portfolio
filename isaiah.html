Isaiah

A patient voice helper for older adults navigating technology, in plain, jargon-free steps, with a hand-off to a real person when needed.

This is a live voice agent. Tap the ‘Start a call’ button at the bottom-right, allow your microphone when asked, and just talk. Speak whichever language is easiest for you. (The voice agent loads only on the published site, not in a preview.)

I
Isaiah · here to help

Tap ‘Start a call’ (bottom-right of the screen) and tell me what is happening with your device. Take your time, there is no rush, and nothing you ask is a silly question.

If you do not see a “Start a call” button at the bottom-right, you are viewing a preview. The live voice agent runs on the published web page, where it can use your microphone.

Prefer a person? Just say “I would like to talk to a real person” and Isaiah will connect you. Anything to do with passwords, accounts, or payments always goes to a human.

Why it matters

  • Before: when technology confuses them, older adults often wait on a busy family member or give up on the task entirely.
  • After: common problems get solved in one patient, plain-language call, and only the small share that truly needs a human, or anything touching accounts and payments, is handed off.

Designed for a vulnerable user, not a power user

  • Ease first: large adjustable type, one obvious button, high contrast, and no time pressure, so it works when someone is anxious.
  • Emotional safety before the fix, one micro-step at a time, never "just" or "simply."
  • A human is always one request away, and passwords, accounts, and payments are always handed to a person.
  • Meets people in their language, English, Spanish, and major Indian languages, spoken back in the same language.
Reasoned impact: the segment most tech support ignores is older adults, who abandon tasks or route every problem to a relative. A calm voice agent that resolves common cases in the moment, grounds each step in real device manuals instead of guessing, and cleanly escalates the rest deflects the "call my kid" moment while keeping reassurance and account-safety human. What stays human: hands-on judgment, anything touching credentials or money, and any caller who simply wants a person.

Isaiah is a designed persona and working prototype. The voice agent runs on ElevenLabs Agents (real-time speech-in and speech-out with one warm neural voice, multilingual, RAG-grounded on real device-support material) with automatic hand-off to a human. For this prototype the human-transfer and callback-scheduling tools are wired to lightweight mock endpoints rather than a paid telephony or scheduling account, so the full workflow can be demonstrated end to end without live billing. Indian-language greetings are AI-drafted and pending a native-speaker review. Built by Malvika Sanghvi.
